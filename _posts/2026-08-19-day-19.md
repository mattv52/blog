I planned to leave at 5 this morning to try get ahead on what was going to be the biggest day yet.

I was awoken by the rain and wind at 4 and decided to check the forecast. Was meant to die down at 6ish. So I updated my alarm and went back to sleep. As I got up around 5:30, all the tradies were up and getting ready to go too.

The sky was cloudy, like always, but this time it was from last night, not today

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034464.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a morning section through some farm land. It was quite nice and the sheep were a bit less hesitant than usual, perhaps because they were just waking up

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034467.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a magpie in the trees that kept swooping as I walked under it. It would fly ahead and wait at the next tree and go again. Like buddy, I'm trying to walk away...

As the sun kept rising, it was quite stunning

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034471.jpg" | relative_url }})

Today is a long day because the only place I could stop is whistling trig. Which is on top of a notoriously windy mountain. It's so windy that the downpipe for the rain water tank is known to sometimes blow out alignment with the tank.

Knowing this was ahead of me, I took a slightly early break at the base where it wasn't yet windy.

After I set off, man was it windy. The tales weren't wrong

Here's a tree that's grown sideways from how windy it is

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034484.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was about 3.5 hours of relentless wind. The only moment of relief was sitting just behind the small tank at the whistling trig "campsite". Due to the notorious nature of it, no one camps there, as they shouldnt. It would have been an awful sleep.

The ridge walk did provide some nice views though, just hard to appreciate in the moment when being blown all over the place

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034477.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034473.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034485.jpg" | relative_url }})

After finally getting over the hill, the trail goes right up near some windmills. Which, given how much I've just complained about the wind, is a great place to have them

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034491.jpg" | relative_url }})

The next few hours were basically spent walking along roads, from one block of windmills to the next. They are enormous things, and quite impressive to be honest.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034497.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034498.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034501.jpg" | relative_url }})

The last 1-2 hours were spent walking on a road into town. I could see it in the distance and it felt like it just wasn't getting any closer.

Side note, why'd the rabbit cross the road?

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034503.jpg" | relative_url }})

Yea, I still have no idea. Just saying what I'm seeing.

Eventually I made it in. Tonights accommodation is an unused train station, that's been renovated and opened up for hikers. There's a sleeping section with wooden bunks, that you put your own mattress on, and a kitchen/lounge area with a sink and table

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034508.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-19/1000034509.jpg" | relative_url }})

It's one of the nicer huts on trail

My knee feels surprisingly good after that. My feet are starting to hurt from though. I think I've worn through the cushion on my sneakers by now.

The long day was brutal, and having one yesterday as well didn't help. Thankfully tomorrow is a nice short one, so there's no rush to get out of bed in the morning
