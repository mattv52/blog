**Stats Summary** 

Hawker to Calabrinda Creek Camp Site

29.1km. 8h 48m. 771m ⬆️ 775m ⬇️

Running Total:

154.4km. 1d 19h 19m. 4227m ⬆️ 4168m ⬇️

While we were in Hawker, we saw that the forecast for Sunday looked pretty grim. 44ml of rain over the whole day and night. At that point we decided we would try to push through to Quorn by Sunday. That gave us about 3 days to cover the ground we had originally planned to take 5. It was going to be some loooong days ahead.

Lucky for us, the gentleman from last night delivered! At 10 to 7 two of them rolled around in their ute. We threw out bags in the back and got rolling. They said they were going to meet up with their friends for breakfast after so it wasn't too much of an inconvenience. But their 15 min detour, saved us 1.5h of walking that we were going to absolutely need later in the day.

So we got cracking at 7:04 with a lovely field walk up to the big ridgeline. Sun rising on our backs

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034061.jpg" | relative_url }})

On the way up, we found a couple baby goats. Goalings? Joey goats? Goeys? Anyway, they were quite cute and surprisingly let us come all the way up to them

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034062.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033810.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then the real climbing began. We were scrambling up the ridge from the side, with massive falls down either side with one wrong move. Photos don't really show how steep the ascent was

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034067.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033814.jpg" | relative_url }})

When we go up there, the views were incredible. Full 360 degrees with massive drops on both sides

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033820.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034064.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033825.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033847.jpg" | relative_url }})

We stopped and had breakfast and enjoyed the view.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033829.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was hard going, our pace was down to 2km/h when it's usually around 3.5-4, but it was so worth it. Highlight of the trip so far.

After the ridge, we headed towards some paddocks and walked through them for a few hours. Initially it was great, we could bang out a heap of KMs real quick, but soon we ended up in a rocky forest on the side of a hill. Pretty rough terrain, hard to see the next marker, and rocks at ever step.

I had this knack of stepping on every loose rock and rolling my ankle constantly, whereas Phil was just looking at directions on his phone without needed to look down

Eventually we cleared the forest and were in wide open country side.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034066.jpg" | relative_url }})

I spotted a lizard just chilling in our path. I was moments from stepping on it, thinking it was just a bit of bark

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033852.jpg" | relative_url }})

By this time, the sun was bearing down on us, and we were very exposed. No trees or anything to provide shade. We decided it was time to pull out the umbrellas for shade and oh wow did they make a difference.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000034065.jpg" | relative_url }})

The irony of needed the umbrellas now for the sun and in a couple days it'll be pouring with rain wasn't lost on us.

Eventually we made it to our camp for the night. After a quick setup, got into bed just in time to see the sun setting

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-6/1000033885.jpg" | relative_url }})

Approaching 9 hours today was a lot. Definitely starting to feel it a little, but the push will all be worth it if we can avoid being outside in the big storm on Sunday
