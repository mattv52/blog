I had a lovely warm sleep in my new sleeping bag. I also realised it was the first time I'd set my tent up since day 7, a whole week ago, Due to being in shelters/accommodation from then.

The morning started walking through forested area, with lots of sheep and lamb grazing

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034335.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then some more sheep in a paddock

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034339.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034341.jpg" | relative_url }})

There were also so weird looking sheep with long necks there too

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034349.jpg" | relative_url }})

I was so focused on following the track, that I took a wrong turn and ended up down a hill I wasn't meant to... Now I had to turn around and come back up. Wasted about 30 mins in the end.

After I eventually got back on track, and went down the right way, I crossed over the valley and started to head up the other side. Due to all the rain, it was all so over grown. You may not even be able to spot the path I was following in this picture

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034353.jpg" | relative_url }})

A good hour was spend pushing through this as I climbed up the hill. At some point, I stepped through and wacked my sore knee (which had been pretty good til then) on a log that was sticking out… It really hurt. I spent most of the rest of the day trying to over compensate for it, taking regular breaks to let it rest.

After breaking through that thick foliage, I made it up onto the ridge section. From here it would be a steady up and down the peaks of this range as I made my way to the next campsite.

Along the way, there was a nice lookout. Over my right shoulder (top left corner) you can see Port Pirie. And the ocean behind it. With the binoculars there, I could see all the way over to Whyalla

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034365.jpg" | relative_url }})

As I kept going along the ridge, I came across a large kangaroo just sitting there on the edge of the path watching me approach. Most others jump away pretty quick, but this guy just sat there.

I was a bit cautious after seeing them fight a few days ago, so I crept up slowly. Eventually he jumped off behind a bush and watched me go

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034376.jpg" | relative_url }})

Over this section I saw a few other hikers. The first on trail in a long time.

One couple just out for the day, a group of 7 doing a small section, a guy who had come from Cape Jervis (he was on day 47!), and a lady who's started in Tanunda because she didn't think she could make it all the way in the time limit she had. It was nice to see fellow hikers

The walk was pretty beautiful, with all the yellow wattle flowering along the side of the path

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-15/1000034378.jpg" | relative_url }})

Gee, it really is yellow season. The canola, wattle and soursobs are all flowering.

The rest of the walk to the camp spot was pretty uneventful. It was a bit of a longer day, with the up and down terrain, and my knee slowing me down a bit. My knees worse than yesterday (because of the big knock) but probably still better than the descent of Mount Remarkable when it flared up first.

The campsite was just me again, and I can hear animals walking/jumping around on the gravel outside my tent. It's a little spooky
