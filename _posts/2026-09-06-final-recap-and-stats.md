Let's start with the stats.

Over the 36 days since I left, I had 3 rest days, 33 walking days.

I covered a total of 1107.5km (over 26 marathons). That's an average of 33.5km a day, or 30.8 if you include the rest days. Shortest day was 17.8km ([Day 20]({{ "/day-20/" | relative_url }})), longest was 58.1km ([Day 25]({{ "/day-25/" | relative_url }})).

I spent 292 hours and 21 minutes walking (over 12 days!). From when I started, to when I finished, that's 34.7% of the time. Its also over 7 FTE (Full Time Equivalent work hours).

I had 32,956m of ascent, which is equivalent to 3.7 Everests or 14.8 Kosciuskos. I also had 33,101m of descent, meaning it was net downhill (by 145m)

I spent 14 nights in my tent, 7 nights in a hut and 11 nights in a hotel and 3 nights at a friend/families house.

I ended up having 13 showers, an average of 1 every 3 days. The longest gap between showering was 3 nights, 4 days, and boy was I ready for a shower after that.

All up, not including gear, the whole trip cost me $2,375.09. This is probably on the upper end of what you would spend to do the Heysen, as I stayed on hotels almost every time and I ate out at every opportunity.

$1,035.25 on accommodation

$580.24 on resupplying in towns

$726.10 on eating out while in town

$33.50 on various little extras, mostly laundry

In terms of animals, I saw stacks goats, sheep, llamas and cows. Also saw too many kangaroos and wallabies to count. 1 echidna, 36 emu, 6 lizards, 1 koala, 6 deer and 0 king cobras (or any snakes for that matter). Yea I really did keep count of all of these.

Overall I really enjoyed my time on the trail. I think I had a good balance of time with people, and time alone.

Would I do it again? Probably not alone, it's a long time and there's lots of other things if rather spend my leave on. But I could be tempted to join a friend if they were keen and wanted company.
