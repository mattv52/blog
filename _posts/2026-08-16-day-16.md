A friend whos been having knee pain reached out and suggested that I should strap my knee for support. I was planning to have a look in the next town (Crystal Brook tonight), but remembered I have a roll in my bag that I use for dealing with blisters. So I watched a quick video then strapped up

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-16/1000034386.jpg" | relative_url }})

It helped a lot more than I was expecting, and happy to say my knee didn't really bother me today. I will say, it hurt a lot ripping it off tonight.

Today was very uneventful. Not really anything to write about. Lot's of road walking, next to green rolling hills

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-16/1000034389.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a short stretch alongside a creek, that was quite nice. Defined path alongside and wasn't running so much that crossing caused an issue. I did dip one of my feet in it accidentally went trying to jump across though.

The only other thing of note was the random lizard statue. Not really sure what this is here for? But it was something different to break up my walk

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-16/1000034393.jpg" | relative_url }})

The trail goes right through the caravan park, so figured I'd stay there the night. After getting in quite early, I watched the end of the cricket, had a shower and then just crashed on the couch til it was dinner time

At 5:30, the time most pubs start serving food, I walked into town to get me some food. Both pubs don't serve food on Sundays… and nothing else was open.

I was pretty disappointed. Had to go to Foodland for my resupply anyway, so just bought a little extra to microwave for dinner tonight.

Overall, it was a fine day. Another pretty easy one in terms of terrain. Pretty samey in terms of views. Plan to have a couple smaller days now on the way to Spalding to hopefully let me know fully recover
