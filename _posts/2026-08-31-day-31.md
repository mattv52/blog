Last night was easily the worst night I've had on trail. I couldn't get comfortable, I don't think I slept for more than an hour block. I think it's partly because my tent was on a pretty big slope in both directions, and partly because I just had an excellent sleep in a cosy house. I was also awake for 2 hours in the middle of the night and couldn't get back to sleep. Hopefully tonight is better.

Because I slept so poorly, I got up early and just head off. There was a nice morning glow through the trees

The start was downhill, which was a nice change for once, but when I got to the bottom, there was lots of water flowing on the paths making them all muddy. This one in particular was a small downpour away from becoming a river

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035118.jpg" | relative_url }})

Today I was heading over Mount Lofty, and on the way back up from the bottom, there was some nice views. I could even see the ocean from here! That's where I'm heading, just the bit that's on the south coast, not west

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035121.jpg" | relative_url }})

But I finally made it!

I kept getting lots of strange looks from just about every person who came past. Like “why's this dude so sweaty and got a massive pack to walk up lofty” kind of looks.

On the way down from lofty, there's a small farm with a big yak looking thing. It's got heaps long cool horns

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035133.jpg" | relative_url }})

Sooooo many of the trails today are super muddy. Most of the time it wasn't too bad to get through, but occasionally it would result in a little bit of muddy shoes

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035137.jpg" | relative_url }})

I walked through the Woodhouse adventure park grounds. On the other side of the site, there's a nice river, which comes from a pond up stream

Through a tunnel

The original plan was to go to mylor today, but that was only another 10k from here and it wasn't even lunch time yet. Because of my early morning, I made really good time and decided to just extend today.

I dropped my bag off at the pub, then went to Coles to get my supplies for the rest of the trip. I think I have everything I need to make it to the end now. Had lunch, packed my supplies and then I headed off a little before 1.

The walk from Bridgewater was along roads, but it was all this kind of trail elevated up next to the road. So it was kind of a nice change

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035152.jpg" | relative_url }})

After a quick stop in mylor to rest my feet, I was off again. It was all road walking for the next 10km. Along proper roads with lots of cars for the first stretch too. At least some of it was behind barriers

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035156.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was such a warm day, and I could see roads ahead so I got the brolly out for another spin. It's amazing how much of a difference the shade makes. And it also allows me to take my hat off. I was a lot cooler

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035161.jpg" | relative_url }})

After the roads, came a farm. This farm was VERY muddy, but thankfully they built some boardwalks through the muddiest parts.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035163.jpg" | relative_url }})

Towards the end of the day, the trail went through an old gold mining area with lots of fenced off mine shafts. Appears there was a big gold rush in Jupiter Creek in the late 1800s

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035164.jpg" | relative_url }})

As I entered the forest area, the sun was coming down just as it was rising the morning, giving a similar golden glow

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035165.jpg" | relative_url }})

At a lot of the small sections that got flooded, past people have built little bridges with whatever they have at hand. Here it is tree bark, often sticks and stones are used too. It's very helpful, thanks previous person.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035166.jpg" | relative_url }})

The sun was really setting as I entered the final stretch of forest before my camp ground. The trees started to look a little eery

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-31/1000035167.jpg" | relative_url }})

Once I finally reached the site, I saw people there. It is a school group… a bunch of rowdy year 11s that haven't been quite since I got here.

How's my luck to find 2 school groups on my thru hike.

While the site is named after the hut, it's not a heysen hut, so you have to pay for it. So I'm just camping nearby in the camp ground area, which is the same as the school group

Today was a long brutal day. I pushed on because I made it to my destination so early, but I only made it there early because I didn't sleep well. And instead of finishing early and sleeping, I kept going. In hindsight it was maybe not the best decision, the last 10k today we're so bad and I was very exhausted at the end, but on the bright side, I now am finishing a day earlier, should be this Saturday. So this is day T-5 to completion!
