It was a pretty good sleep. Full belly, warm room, no rush in the morning.

I spent the first couple hours just chilling on the couch catching up on YouTube.

Then I figured I should actually just get what I needed to done. I emptied out my whole backpack and lay it on the table. Anything that I thought got wet while camping I opened up to let dry.

I hung my tent out on the clothesline to dry and had pretty much everything else scattered in the cabin and hanging on chairs

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-26/1000034872.jpg" | relative_url }})

I went for a stroll into town for a late lunch. One of the pubs had bruschetta on the menu, and for whatever reason I've been craving it since the start.

When I got there, they said their not serving food this week due to renovations… and the other pubs kitched was shut til dinner.

Ended up with a subway footlong, a kransky and an appricot slice. Not as good, but filled the gap in my stomach.

I finally sat down and planned out the last part of the walk. After rejigging it a couple times, and if all goes to plan, I'll be finished on father's day (11 days time!)

It has felt so good to actually get some solid rest today, but I'm very keen to be back out there walking.
