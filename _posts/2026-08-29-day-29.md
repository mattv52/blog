Knowing I had a big day ahead, I set off kinda early. The first hour or so this morning were pretty foggy, especially over the flat area. It was kinda a little bit spooky

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034972.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034976.jpg" | relative_url }})

Pretty much straight away was an ascent up Little Mount Crawford. It was smaller than it's big brother, but still a climb up. The sunrise view from the top was pretty nice though

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034977.jpg" | relative_url }})

There were a few kangaroos around too, enjoying the frosty grass I assume

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034986.jpg" | relative_url }})

Heading over to the more popular camping area in Mount Crawford, the part accessible by car, there's a really nice board walk section

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034991.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000034994.jpg" | relative_url }})

This was the site of my 8th fall…

Being so early in the morning, and it being quite cold, sections were almost icy. The wood was very slippery. As I tend to do when the grind below is a bit slippery, I took a couple step run-up and went for a nice slide. Forgetting I had a 15kg backpack on that's pulling me one way. I failed to adjust correctly and fell over.

I sat there for a minute thinking through what I just did. This one was definitely my fault

In other news, why'd the lamb cross the road?

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000035001.jpg" | relative_url }})

Yea, still not really sure. It seems like animals just like crossing the road

The trail leads through the forest and up to a fire watch look out tower. As to be expected, there's some lovely views from there

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000035007.jpg" | relative_url }})

Most days, I'd drink about 1 litre of water while walking. Knowing this was a big day and it was hot, I packed 2.5L. I had managed to drink all of it was running of vapour. Luckily it wasn't far to the cudlee creek tavern where I could fill up. They also do bruschetta!

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000035058.jpg" | relative_url }})

While it wasn't quite what I wanted, the tomatoes and basil were roasted not fresh, it was quite nice and has filled the gap temporarily until I can get my fix.

The taven is slightly off trail, but I'm happy to walk the extra couple ks if it means some real food.

The stretch into cudlee creek was along a pretty busy stretch of road. Road walking isn't my favourite, and it's much worse when it's along a busy part. There was also a bridge that's barely wide enough for the cars, let alone a person too.

After dinner I walked back up the trail to my hut for the evening

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-29/1000035059.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was a pretty pleasant walk.

The hut is really small. The kind that looks like a kids playhouse. It basically only fits 1 person laying down.

I rolled out my matt, got setup and turned my phone on (its usually on airplane mode all day to save battery). I got a message from a friend who love nearby offering me a bed and shower for the night. As I lay there considering if I can be bothered packing up and walking back to the road, I noticed sooooo many spiders around and took them up on the offer.

It's always nice to be back in a real bed, and even better to get a shower.

Today was a long day. But intentionally so. Knowing there was hot food made it so much more achievable, and the shower and bed was a massive cherry on top.
