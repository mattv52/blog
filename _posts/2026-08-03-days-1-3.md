Parachilna Gorge to Wilpena Pound

For those who don't know, family friend Phil “LuckiePhil” Downing is joining me for these first 2 weeks til Melrose.

![]({{ "/assets/images/days-1-3/1000033194.jpg" | relative_url }})

First 2 days were relatively flat (compared to later in the trail) and we're a great warm in. We've had some great weather, clear blue skys every day.

But it has lead to bitterly cold nights. We had frost on our tents and Phil's trekking poles were frozen on the first morning

![]({{ "/assets/images/days-1-3/1000033208.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/days-1-3/1000033216.jpg" | relative_url }})

Everything has been surprisingly green. There was an enormous amount of rain this year, which has lead to lots of stuff still growing when normally it would be dying out now.

Some of you may have noticed that we are stopping quite early in the day.

Instead of pushing on to the next place (which we probably have time for) we're sticking to schedule and working into the trail. Later on I'll be pushing forward for sure.

Anyway, I don't have much more to say, so here's some pictures
