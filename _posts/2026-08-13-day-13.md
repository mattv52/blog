We were pretty pooped last night. Planned to have a great big sleep in this morning and laze around doing nothing. But as luck would have it I was up at 6:15 and couldn't go back to sleep… we rolled out of bed around 9 and made our way up to the local coffee shop for breakfast. It was really. Melrose is a bit of a BMX Mecca. So the cafe is BMX themed and next to a bike shop.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-13/1000034304.jpg" | relative_url }})

We were talking about the rain that we missed and how it's caused so much damage around us. All the locals everywhere we've been are talking about how much rain they got. So we decided to look it up. At Wilpena (where we stayed on night 3) they got 154mm of rain in 2 days. That soooo much, no wonder all the roads got washed away. Here's a screenshot of the Traffic SA map from tonight (Thursday, 4 days after the rain). Black means road is closed, blue means it's still got water on the road, but possible to get through. We were so lucky to start when we did, and that we got out of that rain. Even over in Port Augusta where we thought it was bad weather, it was nothing compared to what fell further east.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-13/1000034305.png" | relative_url }})

Phil's wife was on her way up to pick him up. And she was also delivering a few bits of gear that I’d asked for. Of those, the most important were a new set of hiking poles, and a new sleeping bag. The ones we have been using (Phil and I have the same) were from Aldi and they were garbage.

In the heat of the moment when I ordered the replacement, I probably over compensated a fair bit and ended up with a -9°C that's quite a bit larger than what I had. Luckily it all still fits in my bag. Hopefully there won't be any more cold nights on the trail.

When we arrived, we went to the brewery for a pizza lunch. The pizzas were quite good. And the place was a really cool little complex.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-13/1000034299.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-13/1000034295.jpg" | relative_url }})

After lunch, Phil left to go back home. So now it's just me, alone til the end.

The final count for falls sits at 9-5. So I have the next 4-5 weeks to fall over less than 4 times.

Since all my socks smelt so bad, I decided I'd just go do a load of laundry while here too. I put on just my rain gear (rain coat and rain pants, nothing else under), so I could put the rest of it in the wash in one load.

It was a nice day to just rest up. All packed up now ready to head off tomorrow morning. Hopefully my knee won't give me any more grief over the next couple days
