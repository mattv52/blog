Hallet to Old Mt Bryan East School Hut

After the big couple days recently, it was nice to have a smaller one. Being small, it also meant there was no rush to leave early.

Got to have a lazy morning in bed, finally heading off around 8:30 (latest the whole walk). Instead of going onto the heysen straight up, I headed up to town, which is slightly off trail. Went to the cafe and got a BLT, Powerade and a couple choc chip cookies. Was a great breakfast.

First part of the day was along a road out of town through some countryside, with a dry creek on one side

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034513.jpg" | relative_url }})

It wasn't long before heading off the road and onto farmland. There were so many sheep around that all scatter as I approached

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034514.jpg" | relative_url }})

The trail followed a fence, which lead up through the farmland and towards a mountain range where Mount Bryan resides.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034516.jpg" | relative_url }})

Excuse the unbuttoned shirt, it was hot

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034522.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was even a mini grand canyon (I guess just canyon?) in the paddock. Scales a bit hard to tell from this photo, but it was only about 2m deep

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034524.jpg" | relative_url }})

There were a couple big climbs to get up the the summit. At the end of the first push, there was a big pack of roos just chilling. Some jumped away pretty soon, others hung around

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034537.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034534.jpg" | relative_url }})

Once up at the summit of Mount Bryan, there was some quite nice views.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034563.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was even a stone chair to over look it

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034556.jpg" | relative_url }})

As I begun my descent, I bumped into a school group on excursion. It was a younger group from Emmanuel. Probably year 7-8's by my guess. I had a quick chat with one of the teachers as I passed by. Thought that was pretty random

The climb down was a lot more steep. And with my knee feeling a bit weaker on the down climbs, I took it nice and slow.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034569.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034572.jpg" | relative_url }})

After the descent, it was road walking through to the hut for tonight, a renovated old school house.

I was pleased to find that this was the official half way point on the trail 🎉

50% rounds up, so I'm pretty much done now :)

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034578.jpg" | relative_url }})

Even from here, Mount Bryan loomed large in the distance

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034581.jpg" | relative_url }})

I was in nice and early and begun to get set up when a bus rolled up. It was the school group from earlier in the day. They were just coming to have a look around thankfully, not staying the night.

After they left, I got settled in, and my mate rolled in a few hours after. He spent a lot longer at the train station this morning.

The sky was clear and the sunset was pretty amazing. The sky turned all sorts of colours. None of which shows up well on camera…

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-20/1000034587.jpg" | relative_url }})

Overall, a pretty easy day. A little hilly, but very short. Got to spend a lot of time resting at the school house.
