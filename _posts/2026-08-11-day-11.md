Phil 8 (+1) - Matt 4

The weather last night was so windy and wild. We could here it against the outside of the shelter all night long.

Initially we planned to leave a little before sunrise to get a head start on the day, but because of the weather, thought it would be wise to wait until sunrise.

After we set out, we pretty much immediately noticed how different the terrain was. It was ragged mountain ranges no more, and lush green rolling hills a plenty. The sun was also starting to poke through the clouds to give us some clear skies again

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034177.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a fair bit of elevation gain and loss today, but not in a single big climb like previously, it was many repeated hills.

After walking for a bit, we stopped and had breakfast on a nice bench over looking the plains. There were some really nice aerial views of the surroundings from up here

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034293.jpg" | relative_url }})

As we headed up and down the hills, heading towards Wilmington (our final destination), we moved away from the big mountains and started getting views of both sides. Eventually we were able to make out Port Augusta in the distance

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034187.jpg" | relative_url }})

On the other side of the hills, we saw large fields of yellow crops. Probably soursobs ready to harvest for the next batch of warheads… or maybe it was canola, who can tell

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034190.jpg" | relative_url }})

It's really hard to explain how windy it was up along these mountains. It was super windy. Pictures won't do it justice, but here's a couple shots that hopefully show something. My hat has a flat rim, but depending on the angle of the wind, it either bent in, or inverted up

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034194.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034197.jpg" | relative_url }})

Towards the end of the day, we had a big push up the final hill before heading down to the road into Wilmington

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034191.jpg" | relative_url }})

Wilmington is a very little town. It doesn't have a whole lot there. But it does have a hotel, with rooms and food.

It's a little off the trail, and doesn't have an official alternate route through it like Hawker and Quorn do, so we decided instead of turning off at the first road, we'd follow the heysen past the town then double back into it so tomorrow wouldn't be as long of a day. This meant we didn't skip any of the trail, and still got to go into Wilmington.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-11/1000034294.png" | relative_url }})

In the end, walking through to Wilmington or walking to Stony Creek Camp Site were about the same distance, except one is on trail, and the other is the wrong direction. So why did we go to Wilmington?

A few reasons.

1. The weather at the moment was a bit unpredictable and we didn't really want to be out there if we could avoid it
2. We both love a good Parmi
3. There's a pay phone in Wilmington. I'm an avid player of payphonetag.com and will be making a few detours along this journey to seek out payphones along the way. This was just the first

In the end, we had a pretty good day. Apart from the wind, the weather was great. We made really good time to get to Wilmington. The hot shower was incredible, and the Parmi was really good too.

We also did our censuses at the hotel too
