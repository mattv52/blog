Today was a rest day, also known as a Zero Day, or Zero. As in zero KMs of trail was done.

The plan from hawker has always been to push really hard to get to Quorn and spend a rest day there, staying out of the rain and wind forecast for Sunday. Due to no hotels being available, we ended up with my uncle and aunt. Which was better in so many ways.

We both have eaten so much today. Our hiker hunger is in full gear now. At every meal the amount of food provided has been incredible and it just kept coming, and we kept eating.

We got our much needed washing done and dried, resupplied what food we needed and dried out our slightly damp head from all the rain yesterday. While waiting for the washing to dry, we planned the next few days into Melrose where Phil will leave me.

We went to their church this evening. It was a really intimate gathering. Usually it's a pretty small group, and the cold weather seemed to make it even smaller. I really enjoyed their style of worship. It was just done from the hymn book, where someone from the crowd would call out a number, we all flip to it and there person on piano starts playing and we all join in.

The longer the day has gone on, the happier we are with the decision we made.
