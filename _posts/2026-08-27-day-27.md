It's a bit of a walk from the caravan park back to the trail. I wasn't in any huge rush either, knowing I'd likely be in accommodation in Tanunda as well.

Decided to head up to the bakery to have breakfast before hitting the trail. As I was going to pay, a random stranger forced a $20 note into my hand and said “You’re on holiday right? My shout”. Then just disappeared before I could refuse. So a bit of trail magic, and breakfast was on her this morning.

Leaving the town, it was a lot of dirt road walking

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034897.jpg" | relative_url }})

Most of the day was spent either on dirt roads like the above, or walking along fences of vineyards like below

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034907.jpg" | relative_url }})

The scenery was quite nice, but I bet looks amazing when it's harvest time for the vineyard.

In one of the farms, I saw a (probably feral) cat. It was kinda a strange sight when it took off. Cats are stuck inside, I've never seen one bolt across a field like that before

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034898.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a random valley which had all these old rusted cars in it. There was probably 3x more than this spread along

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034903.jpg" | relative_url }})

The Heysen will almost always make you follow a fence line. Even if it means a longer route instead of cutting straight across.

This was one of the rare exceptions, it just sent me straight through the middle of the field.

The picture doesn't really show how tall the crops were, but they were all mid thigh high. And wet from morning dew

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034904.jpg" | relative_url }})

I quite quickly realised I wasn't escaping this dry. I rolled my shorts up, held my phone and went. My legs, shoes and socks were all soaked and dripping wet.

It didn't take long to dry out with the sun out, but it was cold

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034906.jpg" | relative_url }})

As I was coming down a bit of a hill, I stepped on a loose rock and fully lost my balance, falling over. I gotta start being more careful, or I'll be catching up to Phil soon

There were more vineyards towards the end of the day, and I'm imagining a few more tomorrow too

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034910.jpg" | relative_url }})

Right at the end, the trail goes over a hill near town and you can see the town. It's always nice to be able to see the end as I'm walking. A bit of an energy boost

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-27/1000034912.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was a bit of a lethargic start today after the rest day. I'm kinda getting quite close to the end now, and I both am happy to be finished but also am enjoying it and don't want it to end. Its a bit of a strange feeling.

Pretty breezy day though. No tough terrain, just a few small hills
