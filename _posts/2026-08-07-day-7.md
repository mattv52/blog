**Stats Summary**

Calabrinda Creek Camp Site to Mount Arden South Camp Site

41.1km. 12h 25m. 1330m ⬆️ 978m ⬇️

Running total

195.5km. 2d 7h 44m. 5557m ⬆️ 5146m ⬇️

We knew this was going to be a huge day, and saw the first stretch was going to be along a dirt road. Which is usually quite easy walking. So we decided to start at 5am. But we were both awake earlier, and so just got going when we finished packing up. Ended up breaking camp at 4:15am.

As we headed along with our head torches on, we came across a pen a sheep, and all their eyes reflected the head torches. Looked pretty spooky

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000033892.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then we found a heysen distance marker. They're scattered all the way along the trail, some in the right spot, others seemingly not. But it's always a good photo opportunity moment.

This one was the 1000km to Cape Jervis sign. Which, all things going to plan, is where I'll end up in a few weeks time

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000033896.jpg" | relative_url }})

We ended up getting the timing pretty perfect. Just as the sun began to rise and our head torches came off, we turned off the road and onto a path.

We had breakfast overlooking a big mountain as the sun was rising. We've had some pretty cool breakfast places these last few days

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034072.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000033901.jpg" | relative_url }})

After crossing over the river we had a lot more exposed countryside to walk through. As the day got on, the sun came up and the umbrellas came out again

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034074.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000033910.jpg" | relative_url }})

There were some pretty impressive mountain faces next to us as we walked along.

We both knew there was a big climb up Mount Arden at the end of the day and so were looking out for which one we thought it was going to be the whole day.

We had lunch at a water tank at the base of Arden, and had the opportunity to dry out my tent which was a bit wet from the dew in the morning.

Then we began the enormous ascent. It was such a big push up, and at the end of a very long day too

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034068.png" | relative_url }})

The brown tent on the left is where we started, the water drop was lunch, and the brown tent on the top right is the end goal.

It began pretty straight forward, but quickly turned into proper scrambling. We followed a creek bed for a large part of the initial ascent, and as it got higher, the steepness increased too

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000033920.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034075.jpg" | relative_url }})

When we finally reached the top of the river climbs, we were up on a chain of mountains. We could see we had to go up and down along then to finally reach the summit of Mt Arden. By this point the weather had changed and the storm was rolling in. It was super windy and overcast now.

From the top, we could see the dry river we sat and ate breakfast at this morning. Ive circled it in red cause it's so far away

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034071.jpg" | relative_url }})

As we got the the final final push up to the summit, there was a car coming down. It was going to incredibly slowly in a really low gear because of how steep it was. And we were about to walk up it…

It took over 20mins and had an average gradient of 18%…

Once we finally got up top, the views were quite nice, but a bit cloudy. The mood was pretty low from the big climb

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034076.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-7/1000034077.jpg" | relative_url }})

I was definitely ready to be finished by this point after being on our feet for such a long time. But we still had to descend part of it to get to the camp ground.

Eventually we made it to camp. I think this was the fastest we've ever setup, we were both just so tired and ready to sleep.

Within a minute of saying goodnight and getting into our tents, the pitter-patter of rain started to fall.

Today was such a long tough day. And tomorrow isn't going to be much easier. But after that we'll be in a nice hotel with real food for a couple days and it'll have been worth it.
