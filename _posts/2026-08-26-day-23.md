LuckiePhil is back!

He's going to be joining me for the stretch from Burra to just before Marschalls Hut. Which will be the final stretch of the Heysen for him 🎉

He only has a few days off before he has to be back at work, so we are on a pretty aggressive timeline. That means today was going to be a long day.

Due to the long day, we decided it would be wise to head off early to ensure we would have plenty of time on trail before it got dark. Because of this, we got treated to a beautiful sunrise

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034679.jpg" | relative_url }})

After the first couple kilometres out of town, we ended up following a fence for a another few kilometres. On one side of the fence was a nice gravel road, on the other was ankle high grass all wet from dew. Take a guess which side we were walking on?

About halfway we decided to just jump the fence and walk on the road.

Once that ended, we were just following a dirt road til the first campsite (which we were skipping)

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034686.jpg" | relative_url }})

On the approach to the campsite, we saw another little scaly lizard guy soaking in the sun

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034688.jpg" | relative_url }})

We had a quick stop at the campsite and ate our lunch, then set off again.

From here we went up a little hill along a dry creek bed, then popped out the top to a pretty good view the whole way around

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034696.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034692.jpg" | relative_url }})

We would basically follow this fence line the whole way across to it

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034707.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034877.jpg" | relative_url }})

Because we made such good time in the end, mainly due to the large chunk of easy road walking to start the day, I got to just lay down on the grass out the front and enjoy some sun for a bit

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034720.jpg" | relative_url }})

Before long I had to get to my daily chores though.

At the end of each day the following have to get done before bed:

- Setup tent (if not in a hut)
- Inflate sleeping pad
- Get changed into camp clothes
- Prepare dinner
- Filter water for the next day
- Prepare anything for tomorrow

It usually take around 1-2 hours to get this all done and most days, the sun is setting by then and it's bed time.

Here's me preparing to filter water into my 3 bottles on the ground

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034878.jpg" | relative_url }})

Before you know it, the sun is setting and it's ready to head for bed

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-23/1000034879.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was a pretty long day distance wise, but a lot of it was spent on roads early on and it turned out to be not so bad. It was pretty cool to get up close to the windmills like that too

The hut was really nice and cosy too
