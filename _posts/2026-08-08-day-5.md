We had a lazy morning expecting to just have a short day into Mayo Hut. Day started out super chill. Some nice, pretty flat walking through the country side.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034054.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then we hit, the now dreaded, river walking section. Walking along dry river beds has to be some of the slowest, most painstaking walking we've done so far.

Some are a lovely slightly soft gravel

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034053.jpg" | relative_url }})

Others are full of stones that are very easy to roll your ankle on if your not careful. Either way, we had to keep jumping over the water flowing down the middle which slowed us down further.

Eventually we ended up walking on the banks alongside whenever possible, even if it meant walking through waist high plants

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034055.jpg" | relative_url }})

As we approached Mayo Hut, we realised how we were going on time and started thinking about pushing onwards. Originally the plan was to do Mayo -\> hawker via the Ridge line tomorrow. But we ended up deciding we could go straight into hawkee tonight, and add the ridgeline walk to the day afters. Basically gaining us half a day.

At the Hut, we sat and had lunch while planning this, and a lady from the census turned up. She was dropping off some forms for anyone who might end up there on census night.

After we left Mayo, it was a long long section of river walking (~12km). Thankfully it was our first overcast day which helped the mood a lot.

Towards the end of the river section, there were some great scenery though

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000033385.jpg" | relative_url }})

Once we got up to the main road, we stopped our trackers (where we left the trail to go into town).

We needed to get to the store before it shut at 5. And it was about a 6km walk after a long day. Since it won't be part of the trail (we will start again here tomorrow), we thought we'd try hitch a ride. Neither of us had tried before, but how hard could it be and who wouldn't wanna pick these lads up?

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000033389.jpg" | relative_url }})

Eventually, we got someone. 12th try. There weren't many cars coming by, so it took about 20mins. We ended up making the store in time and got resupplyed.

At our motel, we did some washing, showered and prepped. We went to dinner at Flinders Food Co on the recommendation of the hiker we shared Yanyanna Hut with. It might have just been the hunger we had, but it was delicious. Highly recommend (if you ever happen to be in hawker)

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034051.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034049.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034050.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-5/1000034048.jpg" | relative_url }})

While at dinner we decided we'd go for another big day tomorrow. Enough that we would be a full day ahead. For that to work, we needed to find a lift back to the trail (6km north of hawker). Lucky for us, LuckiePhil put on his English charm and managed to persuade the table of gentleman next to us to give us a lift out there. Hopefully they turn up 🤞
