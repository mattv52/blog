**Stats Summary**

Melrose to White Park Tank Campsite

35.9km. 8h 30m. 733m ⬆️ 685m ⬇️

Running Total

358.7km. 4d 9h 18m. 11320m ⬆️ 11090m ⬇️

Setting out alone today felt a little strange. I think I'd gotten use to having Phil alongside.

It was a lot of road walking today. Straight out of Melrose was on a dirt road. Alongside some vineyards. The scenery was pretty nice

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034309.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then I turned onto the farm land for the next stretch

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034311.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was a fair bit of walking through wet grass. Which was at times up to my knees. This gave me wet feet for the 4th walking day in a row…

For those who haven't done any of the heysen before (particular over farmland areas) you won't be familiar with these

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034313.jpg" | relative_url }})

These stiles are to let us walkers get over fences. They're particularly common around the middle section through farmlands. Ive been counting them so far, and figure it's a new stat worth adding to the stat summary from tomorrow. This far we've climbed over 32 of them. I had 12 more today, for a total of 44

After climbing over fences through farm land, I got onto the roads on the other side. I would be on these roads pretty much the whole way to my camp site. I just threw my headphones in and plugged along

Along this first stretch of road, there were some sheep wandering around on it.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034315.jpg" | relative_url }})

They were seemingly let out of their paddock. They were running away everytime I got close to them. I ended up "chasing" them a couple km down the road. Hope the farmer doesn't have to get them back there 🤞

Just behind me, I could see Mount Remarkable sticking up. It is quite remarkable how on its own it seems, just don't bother walking to the top

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034318.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a short detour into Murray Town. Just to tag the payphone there (https://payphonetag.com/). But figured I'd stop and have lunch there too.

There are cherry blossoms all around the town, which are in bloom now and look quite lovely

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034322.jpg" | relative_url }})

After returning back to the trail, I walked alongside several canola fields (no, they weren't soursobs unfortunately). They are quite vibrant in colour

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034323.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034327.jpg" | relative_url }})

A caring property owner has setup a little rest spot on the trail, which felt rude to walk past without taking a break. Its amazing how much difference a backrest makes

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034329.jpg" | relative_url }})

Just before getting to camp, there was a look out. From between the mountains, I could just see the ocean.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034330.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-14/1000034333.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was a pretty chill day. Banged out some decent KMs but it was very chill walking. My knee held up alright too which is promising

I was alone at the campsite. Which was nice, but a little eeriy
