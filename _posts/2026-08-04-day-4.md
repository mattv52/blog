### Stats summary

Wilpena Pound to Red Range Camp Site

26.6km. 8h 34m. 779m ⬆️ 839m ⬇️

Running total

94km. 1d 2h 16m. 2846m ⬆️ 2614m ⬇️

It was sooooooooooo cold this morning. Our fingers were numb leaving the Pound. There was frost everywhere.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033324.jpg" | relative_url }})

My beard was catching the moisture from my breath and later froze as well 🥶

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033326.jpg" | relative_url }})

We came across a small pond/puddle that had fully frozen over, so we cracked the surface and played peekaboo with the ice

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033334.jpg" | relative_url }})

We climbed up the saddle between two peaks (Walter hill and Dick Nob. Yes that's really what it's called...). There was an amazing view of the range. We stopped and had breakfast there

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033336.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033786.jpg" | relative_url }})

What comes up, must come down though, and it was a brutal climb down. Incredibly steep and very rocky. Super easy to loose your footing. It did not pass Phil's "Safe to hike alone" check.

At the bottom there was a lot of creek walking, which was pretty slow going, but has some pretty incredible views. We were both just so surprised this was the Flinders Ranges. Everything has been so green and lush. We expected dry, dead and brown.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033342.jpg" | relative_url }})

Out of the creek beds was a sustained incline up to camp tonight. Walking towards the same mountains we had been admiring at breakfast and seeing them get bigger was pretty cool

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033353.jpg" | relative_url }})

At the top of the big push was a nice forest. The shade was absolutely crucial to keeping us cool as the sun was beaming straight down on us at this point. In the forest, we found a father Emu with his 7 chicks in tow

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033357.jpg" | relative_url }})

We finally made it into camp, which was a big push towards the end.

It's a really nice place. Just off a creek, amongst the tall green trees, and it's just the two of us here tonight. Theres a stack of fire wood all stocked up next to he fire pit so we had another fire again tonight. Which is where I'm sitting as I write this, since it's a whole lot warmer than my tent.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033782.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-4/1000033361.jpg" | relative_url }})

Overall it was a pretty long day but some really excellent views. The heat in the second half really wore us out, and the sketchy climb down slowed us quite a bit
