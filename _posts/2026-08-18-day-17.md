The plan today was just a shortish day to Hiskeys Hut. Mainly to test the knee, but also it would be nice to have a shorter day for once. Because I knew it wasn't a long day, there was no rush leaving the caravan park. I rolled out of bed when I was ready, and trundled around packing. Eventually setting off around 7:40. The latest I've left camp yet.

I'd barely been walking 5 minutes from the caravan park, when an elderly man called out across the road "Hey you!" Then waved me over. He wanted to invite me in for coffee and breakfast :)

Him and his wife have lived there for 50 years and he said they often see hikers coming through and bring them in for a chat. It turned out he use to be a diesel mechanic and spent 14 months working in Antarctica!

I had a good chat with them for a while, before finally actually setting off for the day.

Since the trail went right through main street, and there happened to be a bakery opened as I went past (definitely another benefit to leaving a little later this morning), I had to stop for some thing to eat. I ended up with a cheese kransky and a German apricot donut. They were both pretty good

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034401.jpg" | relative_url }})

Is it obvious I enjoyed that?

The forecast for today is a little bit of rain. Nothing major, but a couple showers throughout the day. The sky looked ready to start raining and I spent all day a little bit weary of it.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034403.jpg" | relative_url }})

In the end, I think there was like 2 minutes of an incredibly light sprinkle.

It was lots more road walking today (I think a lot of this section is) which is really easy walking, but usually pretty samey.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034405.jpg" | relative_url }})

There was a couple horses that came over to say hi as I walked past

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034404.jpg" | relative_url }})

The last 5km were off the roads and along the fences of some guys farm. Field and field of green

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034409.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034410.jpg" | relative_url }})

There's a big windfarm being built just north of Spalding. The trail use to go right through it, but has been re rerouted around it for the time being. This is partly why there's a lot of road walking in the section. But as part of this wind farm, they're building a power sub station in this section that I walked past.

On the last stretch into the hut, I came across some kangaroos. It seems one had a Joey half hanging out upside down?

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034415.jpg" | relative_url }})

This huts really nice, 2 big rooms, and a sink with running water. There's a pretty nice view from the toilet too. That's one thing I will miss. Having a great view while on the loo

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-17/1000034421.jpg" | relative_url }})

Caught up to the guy from a few nights ago at Catninga Shelter again. Think we'll be together the whole way through to Burra, provieded our bodies hold up for the long days.

Overall it was a nice change of pace to have an easier day. But I know it's the last shorter day for a couple
