We had looked ahead at the forecast and knew it was meant to rain most of the day today. The next hut was too far away to do in one day, so we had to camp. That meant we'd be setting up in the rain for the first time on this trip. Phil and I had discussed this several times while walking, what you'd do, how to stay warm and dry. Now we would have to put it to the test.

Knowing that once setup, we would just stay in our tents that are dry and not see each other, we decided to have a late start and spend more time at the hut in the morning.

The sky out on a display and gave us a wonderful sunrise

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034721.jpg" | relative_url }})

Eventually, we got started. It hadnt yet started to rain, but it was definitely coming.

The sky was so grey and there was no sign of the sun

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034725.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034883.jpg" | relative_url }})

That field of yellow you can see in the picture above? By the time we got about half way there the rain had properly set in, and our umbrellas were up

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034726.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034729.jpg" | relative_url }})

We were both so impressed by how much of a difference the umbrellas made. The wind was pretty minimal, and the terrain was very open (roads or fields of crops) which allowed us to have them up all day.

The fog had settled in by this point too covering the tops of mountains

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034731.jpg" | relative_url }})

We followed a dirt road for a while. Due to the rain, when walking on certain types of dirt road, the ground would all clump together and stick to our shoes making them wayyy heavier and slide all over the place. We spent most of the time walking through the wet grass on the sides instead.

Along this road we met a farmer. He answered some of our farming questions we had discussed in the days prior and also told us that a few weeks ago they had a whole week of super thick fog. Like 50m visibility which is crazy

After we got off the road, it was a short climb up a hill to the first campsite of the day. We were skipping it and going to the next one, but thought it might provide some shelter that we could stop and have a break to eat our lunch.

Right next to it was this sign

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034884.jpg" | relative_url }})

Excuse the face, it was raining and we kinda just rushed it lol.

My numbers show 19094m of elevation, as you can see in the stats summary, which is obviously more. Ive also had different numbers at each of the distance markers too, so not sure if my gps is inaccurate or theirs is.

After lunch, we went down the other side of the hill, and followed a long section between 2 fence lines. This was my view for most of the afternoon

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034735.jpg" | relative_url }})

The fog/clouds were obscuring the windmills on the hill. If you look really hard you can see the bottoms of them there still.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034737.jpg" | relative_url }})

We walked through a field full of cows. Unlike sheep, the cows don't scatter as you get close, and a few of these had calves nearby. I was a bit apprehensive that they might turn on us. And what are you gonna do if a 500kg cow comes running at you

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034742.jpg" | relative_url }})

In the final couple kilometres, I stepped on a stick that flicked up and got caught between my legs and tripped me over. I landed forward onto my knee. It stung a fair bit. I took this photo the next day, hence the quilt and why it's starting to scab already

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034867.jpg" | relative_url }})

After we got to camp, it was tent up first thing, then I put my bag in the vestibule, so it was out of the rain but not inside my tent (since it was very wet already). It was a bit of a juggling act to get all setup while trying to keep the inside dry, but I think I did pretty well in the end.

By the time we were all setup up and in our tents it wasnt that late. But the fog was quite thick and eerie

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034749.jpg" | relative_url }})

I jerry rigged a clothes line to hang up my shorts, a cloth and my shirt to dry out. And I would need to wear the same clothes tomorrow when the rain continues.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-24/1000034751.jpg" | relative_url }})

Overall it wasnt that bad of a day. Once it started raining, it didn't stop. It rained all day and it is still raining while we sit in our tents. But the umbrellas kept most of our upper body's dry. I made the mistake of putting my backpack down on the wet ground at lunch, and then when I put it back on, it was wet and that soaked through my shorts
