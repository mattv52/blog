It was a nice walk out of Tanunda back onto the trail. It did go through some more vineyards as I expected

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034928.jpg" | relative_url }})

Before long, there was a big push uphill. Ive said it before, but it always feels way harder to do a hill in the morning compared to the after noon.

From the top, there was a nice view of Tanunda

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034932.jpg" | relative_url }})

It wasn't long before I entered Mount Crawford. It was quite pleasant amount the pine trees, and plenty of kangaroos chilling

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034937.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034936.jpg" | relative_url }})

Out of the forest, I went through some more farms. One of them seemed to have the fence fall over and all the cows climbed over to the walking path.

That meant there were heaps of cows all in my way as I tried to follow the path. And they were highland cows too, so they had big scary horns

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034953.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034955.jpg" | relative_url }})

The calves were so cute though

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034958.jpg" | relative_url }})

I made it through slowly, with them all watching me and giving the occasional huff as I went past. I doubt they'd attack, but it wouldn't be pleasant if they did

Passed the 800km marker, with the 300km marker on the other side. That means I only have 300km to go! It's getting close now

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034963.jpg" | relative_url }})

The green hills are really nice to look at, and are starting to feel familiar now

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034965.jpg" | relative_url }})

Ive been to Mount Crawford the forest many times, but today I realised I've never actually been to Mount Crawford the mountain. It was slightly off trail, but thought it was worth going up to the peak since I was close by.

This was right at the end of the day, and I was pretty dead by this point

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-28/1000034970.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was nice to get off the roads today. Most of the day yesterday I could hear cars driving on the roads, which is not really like being in nature. I feel like this next stretch from Tanunda through to myponga will have a fair bit of traffic noises scattered throughout, which is very non existent in the northern end of the trail.

It wasn't too hard today, just a couple big hills. Tomorrow is going to be a killer though. Long day, and more hills…
