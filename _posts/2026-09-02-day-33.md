I had a lazy start this morning, knowing it wasn't a massive day, and I had a room at the end of it. My last for the trip. It turned into a bigger day than I expected though, with the extra bit into and back out of Myponga.

The day started with a fair bit of road walking out of mount compass

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035195.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then into the rolling hills of cattle grazing lands

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035197.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035198.jpg" | relative_url }})

The sky was starting to darken a bit. Some rain was expected today but not too much

As I was approaching, I saw a farmer chasing some cows around in his little buggy. I guess when you don't have a cattle dog, you get a cattle buggy

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035200.jpg" | relative_url }})

At the end of that cattle farm, there was a few cows that seemed to have escaped their paddocks and we're on the wrong side of the fence.

Why'd these cows cross the road?

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035204.jpg" | relative_url }})

It wasn't long before the rain started to set in. At first it was just in the distance, looking like fog on the horizon

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035212.jpg" | relative_url }})

I was following a fence line through another car paddock. And this group of cows were much more active that others. They would all group up Infront of me, and as I walked forward they'd slowly move out of the way and fill back in behind me. Boxing me in

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035219.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035223.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then after a little they'd all run forward and repeat. I think it happened 2 or 3 times in a row. I was a little worried they were going to start closing the circle in on me and force me into the fence

There were a couple little bridges to cross today that were donated and built by the local German club in the early 2000's

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035224.jpg" | relative_url }})

They saved a fair bit of effort to go the whole way down, and a couple were over flowing rivers which would have been a real pain otherwise.

Ive seen this sign around a few times

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035225.jpg" | relative_url }})

It makes me think that some people must just in the spur of the moment decide to follow the heysen and get stuck too far in. I'm also not entirely sure I was experienced when I started, just confidence.

Ive now climbed my 3rd Everest. It was pretty quick after the second. There's been a lot of elevation recently through the Adelaide hills section.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035234.jpg" | relative_url }})

This sign was also the point that I left the trail to head into Myponga. It was like 2pm on a weekday and I walked past a school and the kids were all out playing. One young kid pointed at me and said quite loud to his friend, "Hey look. It's a hiker!" Thought it was pretty random lol

I had 3 purposes for this detour

1\. Capture the pay phone

2\. Get some real food

3\. Buy supplies for my birthday "cake"

Oh yeah, it's my birthday today :)

Step 1, check.

Step 2, check. Had a pizza at the brewery. Semi regret not buying a second takeaway to eat tonight/tomorrow. It was pretty good

Step 3, partial check. The plan was to buy bread, butter and sprinkles and have fairy bread for dinner as my "cake". The only store open was the servo, and they didn't have sprinkles. I ended up buying nerds as a replacement, but in hindsight maybe raw sugar was a better substitute.

The walk from town to my accommodation was only about 5km, but it was a bit awkward carrying my load of bread so it wouldn't be squashed.

There was a couple board walks over some very muddy ground

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035245.jpg" | relative_url }})

And another few Highland cows

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035254.jpg" | relative_url }})

Out the front of my accommodation, they have some sculptures of hikers walking which it thought was pretty cool

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035256.jpg" | relative_url }})

The accommodation is right on the trail, but it's at the back of some guys house. He's built 4 single rooms, each with an ensuite. It's exactly all I needed.

Shortly after I got settled, a second person rolled in.

For some context, in most of the Heysen specific huts, there is a log book you can sign to say you were there. Since we left Burra, so day 23, there's been someone who's only a day or two ahead of me. The whole time. And I've been doing some crazy long days. It's been confusing me so much how I haven't seen her yet. Then bam, she turns up here and I finally meet her. Just the second other hiker heading south that I've met.

Turns out she left the trail for a few days to explore Hahndorf, then just rejoined where she felt like. Which explained how she was always ahead of me, but I could never see her.

Anyway, after we talked for a bit, she found out it was my birthday and insisted on singing happy birthday for me, which was pretty nice

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-33/1000035258.jpg" | relative_url }})

I don't really drink, but there was a free beer in the rooms, and I can't say no to a freebie. You can also see my "fairy bread" with nerds. It was actually alright. Definitely different, but served its purpose. I think I ended up having 2/3 of the loaf before I went to bed

It was a pretty chill day for my birthday. Nothing too hard. It was nice to meet another hiker again, especially her to solve the mystery of why she seemed to just always be ahead of me.
