I set off at 7am, hoping to make good time to the the next hut today.

Starting out from Mount Bryan is along the roads again

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034593.jpg" | relative_url }})

I was preparing for another long day on the roads, before the trail took a sudden turn into a conservation park

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034596.jpg" | relative_url }})

Hoping this creek is the beer, not virus.

The terrain changed dramatically and was starting to look a lot more like the Flinders Ranges again. Which is much more visually stimulating and enjoyable to look at

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034598.jpg" | relative_url }})

The trail followed along the gorge between the mountains which was quite lovely

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034606.jpg" | relative_url }})

Then it went through some flat shrub lands. This scenery is exactly how I picture Australian outback. Redish dirt, small shrubs, eucalyptus trees

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034610.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034608.jpg" | relative_url }})

Part way through this shrub section, there was a sign for the easternmost point of the trail. We had the western most back on day 8, northern is the trailhead in Parachilna and southern will be somewhere near the end along the coast.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034616.jpg" | relative_url }})

It was very savannah feeling in sections too

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034619.jpg" | relative_url }})

Towards the end, the sky got super cloudy. It was a bit strange though, because the sun was still bearing down super strongly.

These 2 photos are taken facing opposite directions. You can see the cloud cover with a small hole for the sun, then the clouds ahead with my sharp shadow

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034620.jpg" | relative_url }})

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034621.jpg" | relative_url }})

When I finally made it to the hut, I was pleasantly surprised to find it was a repurposed portable house. It was quite small, but big enough, and had a sort of cozy feeling. The doorway was quite short though and I managed to hit my head on it several times…

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034622.jpg" | relative_url }})

After about an hour, my mate rolled in too. We both discussed how keen we were for a shower in Burra, since it'd been a fair few long, sweaty days since we last had the opportunity.

While we were chatting, a curious deer came up to the other side of the fence, and just walked back and forth watching us.

![]({{ "/assets/images/day-21/1000034625.jpg" | relative_url }})

Overall, this was the best day since Crystal Brook, maybe even longer. There was a great variety of scenery and the terrain wasnt too tough
